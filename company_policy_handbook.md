# ACME GLOBAL TECHNOLOGIES - EMPLOYEE SEPARATION & RESIGNATION POLICY HANDBOOK
**Document Reference:** HR-POL-2026-SEP  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Last Revised:** January 15, 2026  
**Audience:** All Global Full-Time, Part-Time, and Contract Employees  

---

## Section 1: Purpose and Scope
This policy outlines the official procedures, guidelines, notice periods, and clearance processes required when an employee voluntarily resigns from Acme Global Technologies. All departures must adhere to company standard operating procedures to ensure proper knowledge transfer, data security, and seamless project continuity.

---

## Section 2: Resignation Submission Protocol
1. **Submission Method:** All formal resignations must be submitted in writing via email addressed to `hr@company.com` with a courtesy copy (CC) to the employee's direct reporting line manager.
2. **Effective Notice Initiation Date:** The notice period officially begins on the first business day following receipt and acknowledgment of the written resignation email by the Human Resources Department.
3. **Withdrawal of Resignation:** Any request to withdraw a resignation must be submitted within 48 hours of original submission and is subject to sole management discretion.

---

## Section 3: Notice Period Determinations (Policy Clauses)

### Clause 4.1: Probationary Period Employees (< 6 Months Tenure)
- **Applicability:** Employees actively serving their initial probation period (defined as less than six continuous months from official joining date).
- **Mandatory Notice Requirement:** Exactly **14 calendar days**.
- **Early Relief:** Early release may be approved by Department Head without monetary buyout if knowledge transfer is completed.

### Clause 4.2: Standard Non-Probationary Employees (Tenure 6 Months to 2 Years)
- **Applicability:** Confirmed full-time employees with continuous company tenure between 6 calendar months and up to 24 continuous calendar months.
- **Mandatory Notice Requirement:** Exactly **30 calendar days**.
- **Calculation:** The official Last Working Day (LWD) is calculated as: `Resignation Submission Date + 30 calendar days`.

### Clause 4.3: Tenured Employees (Tenure 2 Years and Greater)
- **Applicability:** Confirmed full-time employees with continuous company tenure of 24 months (2.0 years) or greater.
- **Mandatory Notice Requirement:** Exactly **60 calendar days**.
- **Calculation:** The official Last Working Day (LWD) is calculated as: `Resignation Submission Date + 60 calendar days`.
- **Reasoning:** Extended notice allows thorough handover of critical long-term institutional knowledge, client accounts, and code repositories.

### Clause 4.4: Executive, Director, and Senior Leadership Roles
- **Applicability:** Employees holding titles of Director, Vice President (VP), C-Level Executive, or designated Principal Architects, regardless of tenure length.
- **Mandatory Notice Requirement:** Exactly **90 calendar days**.
- **Special Conditions:** Subject to mandatory Garden Leave provisions upon mutual agreement.

---

## Section 4: Handover, Clearance, and Asset Return Guidelines

### Clause 5.1: Knowledge Transfer (KT) & Documentation
1. Resigning employees must compile a complete Handover Document within 7 business days of resignation approval.
2. Mandatory walkthrough sessions must be scheduled with designated replacement team members.
3. Direct line manager must sign off on the Knowledge Transfer Checklist before final clearance is issued.

### Clause 5.2: Company Asset Return
1. All company-owned property (including laptops, security badges, corporate cards, mobile devices, and access tokens) must be surrendered to IT/Facilities on or before 4:00 PM on the official Last Working Day.
2. Failure to return company property will postpone final settlement (Full & Final Settlement - FNF).

### Clause 5.3: Leave Encashment and Final Settlement (FNF)
1. Accrued and unused earned leaves (up to a maximum cap of 18 days) will be encashed and paid out in the FNF cycle.
2. Final settlement statement and experience certificates are processed within 30 calendar days of the official Last Working Day.

---

## Section 5: Notice Period Waiver & Buyout Policy
1. Notice waivers can only be granted upon written recommendation of the Department Vice President and explicit approval by the Head of HR.
2. In cases of unserved notice periods without approved waiver, notice buyout will be calculated based on standard daily basic gross salary.
